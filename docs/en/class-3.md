##  Lesson Content

### The Properties of Good Money

Throughout history, humans have used many things as money: shells, beads, salt, gold. The best forms of money share certain properties:

| Property | Definition | Bitcoin |
|----------|------------|---------|
| **Scarcity** | Limited supply |  21 million max |
| **Durability** | Doesn't degrade |  Digital, eternal |
| **Divisibility** | Can be split |  100M sats per BTC |
| **Portability** | Easy to move |  Borderless |
| **Fungibility** | Units are equal |  1 BTC = 1 BTC |
| **Verifiability** | Easy to verify |  Nodes verify |

### Absolute Scarcity: 21 Million

Bitcoin is the first asset in human history with **absolute scarcity**. There will only ever be 21 million Bitcoin:

- No government can print more
- No company can issue more shares
- No one can change the supply

### Stock-to-Flow Model

The **Stock-to-Flow (S2F)** ratio measures scarcity:

- **Stock** = existing supply
- **Flow** = new production per year
- **S2F** = Stock  Flow

| Asset | S2F Ratio |
|-------|-----------|
| Gold | ~62 |
| Bitcoin (post-2024) | ~120 |
| Silver | ~22 |

After the 2024 halving, Bitcoin became the hardest money ever created.

### Sound Money vs. Fiat Money

| Sound Money (Bitcoin) | Fiat Money (Dollar) |
|----------------------|---------------------|
| Fixed supply | Unlimited supply |
| Decentralized | Centralized |
| Transparent rules | Arbitrary decisions |
| Deflationary | Inflationary |
| Permissionless | Permission required |

##  Key Takeaways

1. **Good money has specific properties**: scarcity, durability, divisibility, portability, fungibility, verifiability
2. **Bitcoin has absolute scarcity**: only 21 million will ever exist
3. **Stock-to-Flow measures hardness**: Bitcoin is now harder than gold
4. **Bitcoin is sound money**: fixed rules, no central authority
5. **Fiat fails as money** due to unlimited supply and centralized control
