### The Halving

Every **210,000 blocks** (~4 years), the block reward is cut in half:

| Halving | Year | Reward |
|---------|------|--------|
| Genesis | 2009 | 50 BTC |
| 1st | 2012 | 25 BTC |
| 2nd | 2016 | 12.5 BTC |
| 3rd | 2020 | 6.25 BTC |
| 4th | 2024 | 3.125 BTC |
