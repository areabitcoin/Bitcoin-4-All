##  Lesson Content

### Why Withdraw from Exchanges?

Remember: **"Not your keys, not your coins."**

Exchanges are convenient for buying, but they're not safe for storing:
- Exchange hacks have lost billions
- FTX collapsed overnight, users lost everything
- Governments can force exchanges to freeze accounts
- You don't truly own Bitcoin until you self-custody

### Step-by-Step: Withdrawing Bitcoin

#### Step 1: Set Up Your Wallet

Before withdrawing, you need a wallet ready:

1. **Choose a wallet** (BlueWallet, Sparrow, or hardware wallet)
2. **Install and create** a new wallet
3. **Write down your seed phrase** on paper
4. **Verify your backup** by restoring the wallet
5. **Generate a receive address**

#### Step 2: Get Your Receive Address

In your wallet:
1. Tap/click "Receive"
2. Copy the Bitcoin address (starts with bc1... or 3... or 1...)
3. **Double-check** the address - mistakes are irreversible!

#### Step 3: Initiate Withdrawal on Exchange

On your exchange:
1. Navigate to "Withdraw" or "Send"
2. Select Bitcoin (BTC)
3. Paste your wallet address
4. **Verify the address** matches exactly
5. Enter the amount
6. Review the network fee
7. Confirm and complete 2FA

#### Step 4: Wait for Confirmation

- Transactions need **confirmations** (blocks)
- 1 confirmation: ~10 minutes
- 3 confirmations: considered safe for most amounts
- 6 confirmations: highly secure

#### Step 5: Verify Receipt

Check your wallet to confirm the Bitcoin arrived. Congratulationsyou now have sovereign Bitcoin! 

### Pro Tips

-  **Start small** - test with a small amount first
-  **Triple-check addresses** - copy/paste, then verify
-  **Whitelist addresses** - some exchanges allow this for security
-  **Consider timing** - fees are lower on weekends
-  **Batch withdrawals** - combine to save on fees

##  Key Takeaways

1. **Withdrawing to self-custody is essential** for true ownership
2. **Always test with small amounts first**
3. **Triple-check addresses** before confirming
4. **Wait for confirmations** before considering it complete
5. **Congratulations!** You now have sovereign Bitcoin
