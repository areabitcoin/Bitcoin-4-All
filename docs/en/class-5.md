##  Lesson Content

### Mining: Securing the Network

**Bitcoin mining** is the process of adding new blocks to the blockchain. Miners:

1. Collect pending transactions into a block
2. Race to solve a cryptographic puzzle
3. The winner broadcasts their block to the network
4. They receive the **block reward** + transaction fees

Mining requires specialized hardware (ASICs) and consumes electricitythis is the "cost" that makes Bitcoin secure.

### Proof of Work: The Consensus Mechanism

**Proof of Work (PoW)** is how Bitcoin achieves consensus without a central authority:

- Miners must prove they did computational "work"
- This work requires energy expenditure
- It's easy to verify but hard to produce
- Makes attacking the network extremely expensive

> "Proof of Work converts energy into truth." - Unknown

### The Halving: Programmed Scarcity

Every **210,000 blocks** (~4 years), the block reward is cut in half:

| Year | Block Reward | Total BTC Mined |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| 2009 | 50 BTC | 0 |
| 2012 | 25 BTC | 10.5M |
| 2016 | 12.5 BTC | 15.75M |
| 2020 | 6.25 BTC | 18.375M |
| 2024 | 3.125 BTC | 19.6875M |
| 2140 | 0 BTC | 21M |

### Bitcoin Market Cycles

Bitcoin historically follows **4-year cycles** aligned with halvings:

1. **Halving** - Supply shock occurs
2. **Bull Market** - Price rises dramatically (~12-18 months)
3. **Blow-off Top** - Euphoria peaks
4. **Bear Market** - Price corrects 70-85%
5. **Accumulation** - Smart money buys
6. **Repeat**

Understanding cycles helps with investment timing.

##  Key Takeaways

1. **Mining secures the network** by adding computational cost
2. **Proof of Work converts energy into security** - makes attacks expensive
3. **The halving occurs every 4 years** - cutting block rewards in half
4. **Only 21 million BTC will ever exist** - last one mined around 2140
5. **Bitcoin follows 4-year cycles** aligned with halving events
