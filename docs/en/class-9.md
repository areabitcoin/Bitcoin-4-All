##  Lesson Content

### "Not Your Keys, Not Your Coins"

This famous saying captures Bitcoin's most important principle: **self-custody**.

When you leave Bitcoin on an exchange:
- The exchange controls your Bitcoin
- They can freeze your account
- They can be hacked
- They can go bankrupt (FTX, Mt. Gox)

When you self-custody:
- **You** control your Bitcoin
- No one can freeze it
- No one can take it
- True financial sovereignty

### What is a Bitcoin Wallet?

A wallet doesn't actually "store" Bitcoin. Bitcoin exists on the blockchain. A wallet stores your **private keys**the cryptographic proof that you own certain Bitcoin.

### Types of Wallets

| Type | Security | Convenience | Best For |
|------|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Exchange** | Low | High | Trading only |
| **Hot Wallet** | Medium | High | Daily spending |
| **Cold Wallet** | High | Medium | Long-term savings |
| **Hardware Wallet** | Very High | Medium | Serious holders |
| **Multi-sig** | Highest | Low | Large amounts |

### Hot Wallets vs. Cold Wallets

**Hot Wallets** (connected to internet):
- Mobile apps: BlueWallet, Muun, Phoenix
- Desktop: Sparrow, Electrum
- Good for smaller amounts

**Cold Wallets** (offline):
- Hardware devices: Trezor, Ledger, Coldcard, Bitbox
- Air-gapped computers
- Essential for larger holdings

### The Seed Phrase (Recovery Phrase)

Your **seed phrase** is 12 or 24 words that can recover your entire wallet:

 **CRITICAL RULES:**
1. **Write it down on paper** - never digitally
2. **Store in multiple secure locations**
3. **Never share with anyone**
4. **Never enter online**
5. **Test your backup** before storing significant amounts

> Whoever has the seed phrase controls the Bitcoin.

##  Key Takeaways

1. **"Not your keys, not your coins"** - self-custody is essential
2. **Wallets store private keys**, not Bitcoin itself
3. **Use hot wallets for spending**, cold wallets for savings
4. **Hardware wallets are recommended** for significant amounts
5. **Protect your seed phrase** like your life depends on it
